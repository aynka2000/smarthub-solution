Media and Entertainment

Consumer consumption of physical as well as digital content and media has morphed, bringing with it a host of new challenges and opportunities. Powerful online communities are pushing news and events into real time viral storms. New distribution channels and partnerships are creating unprecedented business opportunities. Two trends clearly impact the media and entertainment industry. These trends are digitization and convergence. They both compel companies to create new business models and innovative approaches to growth.

In rapidly changing markets, Media & Entertainment companies must redefine the customer experience, capitalize on digital convergence and quickly launch new business models. SmartHub Solution delivers innovative business and technology solutions that enable you to gain agility in new product development, manage costs in business operations and derive insight from performance data, helping you meet the needs of customers today and tomorrow.

SmartHub Solution’s expertise enable broadcasters, publishers, gamers, out-of-home services, advertising agencies and sports/ entertainment businesses to quickly adapt and align to customer needs. Our global Media & Entertainment center of excellence (CoE) helps broadcast, publishing, and entertainment organizations innovate and stay ahead of the market. Whether implementing a new business process and solutions for an operational and content management system; or building and operating a digital supply chain solution, you can depend on our experts. We collaborate with you to profitably grow your business today and open new business opportunities.
