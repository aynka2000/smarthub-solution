Integrated Services

Today’s Enterprise IT landscape comprise of numerous heterogeneous technologies, applications and packaged software products originating from various vendors, which creates unnecessary complexities and redundancies that prevents the automation of even the simplest of processes. Multi-vendor IT environments create many challenges with different levels of process maturity and a lack of process alignment between IT lifecycle phases hinder delivery speed and time to market. IT leaders need to run core operations in an extremely nimble fashion while focusing continuously on reducing cost and investing more in ‘grow-the-business’ initiatives.

Smarthubsolution’s application Integration Service is the way forward for enterprises to integrate legacy applications, hardware systems, business processes and modern technologies such as Virtualization, Cloud etc. We leverage MuleSoft, Azure BizTalk, TIBCO, Boomi, Informatica and modern integration platforms to develop API-based integration frameworks and application networks, helping businesses change their pace of innovation.
